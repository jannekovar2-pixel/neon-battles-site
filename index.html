What it is

Neon Battles is a daily short video: two neon fighters, each with its own ability or medieval weapon, fight in a circular arena until one is left. Every fight is a real simulation, nothing is scripted, and everything in it is made by code: the physics, the graphics and the sound.

Season 1 is a knockout bracket of 16 fighters. One fight a day, losers are out, one champion is left.
